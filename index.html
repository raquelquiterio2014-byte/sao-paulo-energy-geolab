Methodology & data quality

  • Coordinates: WGS84 longitude/latitude. Distances: ellipsoidal geodesics.
  • Dates are source dates supplied by the importer, not independently verified.
  • Imported does not mean verified or complete.
  • No power-flow, congestion or investment-feasibility inference.
  • Different IDs are not automatically deduplicated across inventories.
  • Coordinate and state formats are checked; state-boundary containment is not validated.
